# CurseForge listing materials

## Project name

QuestLore

## Summary

Narrative context for your active quest, in English and Spanish, with race/class introductions and a personal story archive.

## Description

QuestLore explains **why your character is doing a quest**, using short narrative summaries in English or neutral Spanish.

Select a quest in your quest journal or tracker to read its context in a transparent Lore section below the quest list. The current quest title appears in yellow and the story in white. Minimize the section when you need room, adjust text size in the addon options, or detach and resize the window.

### Features

- 5,259 curated bilingual quest narratives across the audited Forever catalog, including Forever-specific quests.
- Stories focused on information available at the current quest step, without importing later chain outcomes.
- Integrated race/class introductions for new level-one characters.
- Automatic game-language detection, plus manual English and Spanish choices.
- Up to 200 unique saved stories per character, with a separate archive in the addon options.
- Per-character font size, attachment, window position, dimensions, and minimized state.
- Local narrative data: no online AI or translation service during play.

### Compatibility and installation

Built for **World of Warcraft: Forever, interface 16001**. Retail and other Classic clients are not verified. Extract the QuestLore folder into `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`. Restart for a new installation or use `/reload` after an update.

**ForeverCore** is optional and enables attachment below the quest tracker. Without it, Lore works as an independent window. Polestar is optional. Enable or disable **Follow Polestar's active quest** in QuestLore options to follow its accepted navigation quest directly. The setting defaults to on and is saved per character.

Open **Options → AddOns → QuestLore** for language, text size, attachment, and story history. `/lore` shows the window.

The catalog audit includes a small number of supplementary records whose current availability is unverified; catalog coverage does not guarantee every quest is currently obtainable. Unknown or changed quests can use available quest-text fallback instead of a curated story.
